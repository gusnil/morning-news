'''Super Bowl LXI''' is the upcoming [[American football]] championship game of the [[National Football League]] (NFL) for the [[2026 NFL season|2026 season]]. The game will be played on February 14, 2027, at [[SoFi Stadium]] in [[Inglewood, California]]. This will be the ninth [[Super Bowl]] hosted by the [[Greater Los Angeles]] area, and the second at this venue, the first being [[Super Bowl LVI]] in 2022. It will also be the first Super Bowl to be played on both [[Valentine's Day]] and [[Presidents' Day|Presidents' Day weekend]].

The game is set to be nationally televised by [[American Broadcasting Company|ABC]] and streamed on [[Disney+]] and the [[ESPN (streaming service)|ESPN app]], with the main broadcast and four [[Altcast|altcasts]] also being carried by [[ESPN]], [[ESPN2]], [[ESPNU]], [[NFL Network]], and [[Disney Channel]]/[[Disney XD]] respectively. It will mark ABC's first Super Bowl since [[Super Bowl XL]] in 2006, and the first time the main ESPN networks or NFL Network will carry a live Super Bowl.

=== Host selection ===
The league has made all decisions regarding hosting sites from [[Super Bowl LVII]] onward. There is no bidding process per site: the league selects a potential venue unilaterally, the chosen team puts together a hosting proposal, and then the league votes to determine whether it is acceptable.<ref>{{Cite web |last=Teope |first=Herbie |date=May 23, 2018 |title=Arizona, New Orleans chosen as Super Bowl hosts |url=https://www.nfl.com/news/arizona-new-orleans-chosen-as-super-bowl-hosts-0ap3000000933935 |access-date=2025-02-28 |website=NFL.com |archive-date=February 2, 2020 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20200202190059/http://www.nfl.com/news/story/0ap3000000933935/article/arizona-new-orleans-chosen-as-super-bowl-hosts |url-status=live }}</ref>   
 
On December 13, 2023, the NFL announced that [[SoFi Stadium]], home of both the [[Los Angeles Rams]] and the [[Los Angeles Chargers]], was selected as the Super Bowl site.<ref>{{cite web|last=Jones|first=Jonathan|title=Super Bowl expected to return to SoFi in 2027: Los Angeles area to host big game for second time since 2022|url=https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/super-bowl-expected-to-return-to-sofi-in-2027-los-angeles-area-to-host-big-game-for-second-time-since-2022/|work=CBS Sports|date=December 13, 2023|access-date=December 13, 2023|archive-date=December 13, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231213204821/https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/super-bowl-expected-to-return-to-sofi-in-2027-los-angeles-area-to-host-big-game-for-second-time-since-2022/|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{cite magazine|last=Chavkin|first=Daniel|title=SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles Expected to Host Super Bowl LXI, per Report|url=https://www.si.com/nfl/2023/12/13/super-bowl-61-sofi-stadium-rams-chargers-host|magazine=Sports Illustrated|date=December 13, 2023|access-date=December 13, 2023|archive-date=December 13, 2023|archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20231213205423/https://www.si.com/nfl/2023/12/13/super-bowl-61-sofi-stadium-rams-chargers-host|url-status=live}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |last=Schefter |first=Adam |author-link=Adam Schefter |date=2023-12-13 |title=Super Bowl LXI to be played at SoFi Stadium in L.A. |url=https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/39104280/sources-super-bowl-lxi-played-sofi-stadium-la |access-date=2023-12-14 |website=ESPN.com |archive-date=December 4, 2025 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20251204052602/https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/39104280/sources-super-bowl-lxi-played-sofi-stadium-la |url-status=live }}</ref> This will be the ninth Super Bowl hosted in the [[Greater Los Angeles]] Metropolitan Area, with SoFi Stadium last hosting [[Super Bowl LVI]] in 2022.

===Logo===
The official logo for Super Bowl LXI was unveiled by the game's broadcaster [[ESPN]] on February 8, 2026, during a special post-Super Bowl LX edition of ''[[SportsCenter]] with [[Scott Van Pelt]]'' from SoFi Stadium. It was formally unveiled during a press conference in Los Angeles the following day, accompanied by an introductory video narrated by Inglewood-based rapper [[D Smoke]]. Keeping with the tradition established at Super Bowl LVI, the logo features [[Roman numerals]] with imagery representing the host city/region, in this case being [[Wind wave|waves]] – reflecting Southern California's [[surfing culture]] and the "grit and tenacity of Los Angeles".<ref>{{Cite news |last=Erzberger |first=Tyler  |date=February 9, 2026 |title=ESPN might have leaked the Super Bowl LXI logo—See it here first |url=https://www.newsweek.com/sports/nfl/espn-might-have-leaked-the-super-bowl-lxi-logo-see-it-here-first-11487734 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260209060918/https://www.newsweek.com/sports/nfl/espn-might-have-leaked-the-super-bowl-lxi-logo-see-it-here-first-11487734 |archive-date=2026-02-09 |access-date=2026-02-10 |work=Newsweek |language=en-US}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2026-02-09 |title=Logo unveiled for Super Bowl LXI at SoFi Stadium |url=https://ktla.com/sports/logo-unveiled-for-super-bowl-lxi-at-sofi-stadium/ |access-date=2026-02-09 |website=[[KTLA]] |publisher=[[Nexstar Media Group]] |archive-date=February 10, 2026 |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260210170443/https://ktla.com/sports/logo-unveiled-for-super-bowl-lxi-at-sofi-stadium/ |url-status=live }}</ref><ref>{{cite web | last=Breech | first=John | title=Super Bowl LXI logo unveiled: Colors spark theories for 2027 Valentine's Day game | website=CBS Sports | date=2026-02-09 | url=https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/super-bowl-lxi-logo-unveiled-valentines-day-2027-los-angeles-sofi-stadium/ | access-date=2026-02-10 | archive-date=February 10, 2026 | archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260210105144/https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/super-bowl-lxi-logo-unveiled-valentines-day-2027-los-angeles-sofi-stadium/ | url-status=live }}</ref>
