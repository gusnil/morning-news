The '''2027 UCI World Tour''' is a series of races that includes thirty-six events throughout the [[2027 in men's road cycling|2027 cycling season]].<ref>{{cite news |title=UCI reveals 2027 WorldTour racing calendars with 'Super' World Championships in France forcing changes |url=https://www.cyclingnews.com/pro-cycling/racing/uci-reveals-2027-worldtour-racing-calendars-with-super-world-championships-in-france-forcing-changes/ |access-date=20 October 2026 |agency=Cyclingnews |date=6 June 2026}}</ref> The tour will start with the [[2027 Tour Down Under|Tour Down Under]] on 19 January and will conclude with the [[2027 Tour of Guangxi|Tour of Guangxi]] on 17 October.

== Events ==
The most significant change compared to the [[2026 UCI World Tour|2026 calendar]] was the rescheduling of the [[2027 Vuelta a España|Vuelta a España]], which moved from its traditional late-August start to 4 September. This change was made to accommodate the [[2027 UCI Road World Championships|UCI Road World Championships]], part of the second edition of the UCI "Super World Championships", held in [[Haute-Savoie]], France, from 24 August to 5 September 2027.<ref>{{Cite web |title=2027 UCI Cycling World Championships Haute-Savoie Mont-Blanc |url=https://www.uci.org/event-hub/2027-uci-cycling-world-championships/3eK8O5bGa5sfvNlGaTPCb3 |website=UCI |access-date=20 October 2026}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |title=Discover the calendar for the 2027 UCI Cycling World Championships in Haute-Savoie Mont-Blanc |url=https://www.uci.org/pressrelease/discover-the-calendar-for-the-2027-uci-cycling-world-championships-in-haute-savoie-mont-blanc/1tEIUtEoRq13aaef4p3bdx |website=UCI |access-date=20 October 2026}}</ref>
