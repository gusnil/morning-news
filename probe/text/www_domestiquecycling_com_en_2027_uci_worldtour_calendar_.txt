 2027 UCI WorldTour calendar
 The 2027 UCI WorldTour calendar maps out another year of racing across the globe. Spanning 36 races in 13 countries across four continents, the 2027 men’s WorldTour will cover 169 days of competition.
 The season opens in January with the Santos Tour Down Under in Australia from 19 January and closes on 17 October with the Tour of Guangxi in China, in a schedule reshaped by the 2027 UCI Cycling World Championships.
 Haute-Savoie Mont-Blanc will host the 2027 UCI Cycling World Championships from 24 August to 5 September, prompting La Vuelta Ciclista a España to return to a September-only slot from 4 to 26 September.
