 Grand Sumo Tournament Calendar for 2026-2027
 Upcoming Tournaments
 Grand Sumo Tournaments (本場所, honbasho) are held six times a year, once every odd-numbered month.
 Each tournament lasts for 15 days, starting on the second Sunday of the month.
 Three tournaments are held at the Ryōgoku Kokugikan in Tokyo (January, May, and September),
 and one each in Osaka (March), Nagoya (July), and Fukuoka (November).

 Schedule Information
 Advanced Ticket Sales
 Around five weeks before each tournament begins, tickets will be made available. All 15 days go on sale 
 at the same time and popular days (especially weekends) sell out quickly. If you intend to purchase tickets
 to a very popular day, remember that the dates shown are based on Japan Standard Time. 
 More about ticket purchases -->
 Banzuke Announcement
 The banzuke (official ranking list) is typically announced about two weeks before each
 tournament. This determines the match-ups and rankings of all wrestlers participating
 in the tournament.
 Learn about rankings -->
 Tournament Structure
 Each grand tournament (honbasho) lasts for 15 days, with wrestlers in the top two
 divisions fighting once each day. The wrestler with the best record at the end of
 the 15 days is declared the tournament champion.
