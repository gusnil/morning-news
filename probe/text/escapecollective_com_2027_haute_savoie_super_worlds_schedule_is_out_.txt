 2027 Haute-Savoie Super Worlds schedule is out
 A year out from the second edition of the UCI Cycling World Championships, the UCI has released the competition schedule . The Haute-Savoie department is picking up the mantle from Glasgow, Scotland, which held the first 'Super Worlds' in August 2023, combining all cycling and para-cycling disciplines except cyclocross in one two-week event.
 The second Super Worlds will take place from 24 August-5 September 2027, during which time 288 rainbow jerseys will be handed out across 16 venues. While most events will take place in the Haute-Savoie, the track cycling competition will run at the Paris Olympic venue at Saint-Quentin-en-Yvelines.
 The full schedule can be found here (pdf) , but some of the key events are as follows:
 26-27 August (Thursday-Friday) – Gravel
 26-28 August (Thursday-Saturday) – Downhill MTB
 30 August-4 Sept (Monday-Saturday) – Cross-Country MTB
 30 August-2 Sept (Monday-Thursday) – Time Trials
 25-29 August (Wednesday-Sunday) – Age-group and Para-Cycling Road Races
 29 August (Sunday) – Elite Men's Road Race
 5 September (Sunday) – Elite Women's Road Race
 26-30 August (Thursday-Monday) – Track Racing
 1-5 September (Wednesday-Sunday) – Para Track Cycling
